# Product Decision Sheet

- Business shape: one Tutor.
- Scheduling model: direct Booking.
- Student funding: Session Credits only.
- Initial balance: one credit at Invitation Claim.
- Replenishment: once when a Student-funded Booking becomes Past or is cancelled.
- Complimentary Booking: Tutor-created; no credit effect.
- Upcoming limit: one per Student.
- Student reschedule cutoff: 24 hours.
- Delivery: Invitation Links and Login Links are manual.
- Notification: existing Next Booking dashboard card only.
